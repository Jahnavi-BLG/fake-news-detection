🔍 Metric Definitions & Significance

Accuracy (98.73%)

Measures the proportion of total news articles (both Real and Fake) correctly classified by the model across the test set.

Precision (98.43%)

Measures out of all articles predicted as REAL NEWS, what percentage were genuinely authentic news stories.

Recall (99.25%)

Measures out of all actual real news articles present in the test set, what percentage were successfully identified by the AI model.

F1 Score (98.84%)

The harmonic mean combining Precision and Recall into a single robust benchmark metric, ensuring balanced model performance.
